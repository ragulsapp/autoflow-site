Clinics & Doctors · Chennai, Tamil Nadu

A multi-location clinic group in Chennai

A WhatsApp appointment-booking bot that replaced manual, phone-based scheduling across multiple doctors and locations.

AutoFlow Botonline

The problem

Booking by phone doesn't scale

  • —Reception staff were spending hours a day taking appointment calls, checking doctor availability by hand, and confirming bookings one at a time.
  • —With several doctors and locations to track, double-booking and missed reminders were a recurring problem.

The build

What we built

  • A state-machine WhatsApp bot that walks patients through choosing a doctor, location, and time slot, entirely inside a WhatsApp conversation.
  • Live availability tracked in Google Sheets, so reception staff always see the same data the bot is booking against.
  • Automatic confirmation messages the moment a slot is booked, with reminders ahead of the appointment.

The result

The clinic group now runs day-to-day appointment booking through WhatsApp instead of phone calls, across multiple doctors and locations, on a live paid retainer.
