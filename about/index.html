About AutoFlow

A one-person automation studio, built for Chennai businesses

AutoFlow is run out of Chennai by Ragul Kumar, building WhatsApp-first automation for Tamil Nadu businesses — salons, clinics, restaurants, real estate agents, coaching institutes, and e-commerce sellers who are losing hours a day to manual booking, lead follow-up, and customer updates.

Every automation is built on n8n, self-hosted, connected to your own WhatsApp Cloud API number and a plain Google Sheet — not a black-box SaaS dashboard you rent forever. If you ever want to walk away, your data and your business logic go with you.

Why not just resell AiSensy or WATI

Because a reseller can't build you an AI agent

Most WhatsApp automation in Tamil Nadu is a resold platform subscription with a markup on top. That gets you broadcast messages and simple click-to-reply menus. What it doesn't get you:

  • —A custom AI conversation layer — most Chennai resellers sell click-to-reply bots, not AI.
  • —Multi-source lead aggregation across IndiaMART, JustDial, Facebook, and your website in one flow.
  • —A genuinely conversational Tamil-language AI agent, not just Tamil broadcast messages.
  • —Business-specific logic like COD confirmation, Tally sync, or GST invoicing.
  • —Transparent pricing — most resellers mark up the underlying platform cost by 200–300%.
